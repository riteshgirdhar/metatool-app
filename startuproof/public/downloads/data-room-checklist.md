# Seed data-room checklist

Investors' requests vary; confirm what is needed with your advisers. Upload only what you have reviewed.

## Company
- [ ] Certificate of incorporation / trade licence and constitutional documents
- [ ] Current cap table (fully diluted) and option pool details
- [ ] Shareholder agreements and prior investment documents
- [ ] Board and shareholder resolutions

## Product and traction
- [ ] Product overview or demo video
- [ ] Key metrics with definitions (monthly, last 12–24 months)
- [ ] Cohort / retention data
- [ ] Top customer list and material contracts

## Financial
- [ ] Monthly management accounts
- [ ] Financial model with assumptions
- [ ] Bank statements (recent months) if requested
- [ ] Use of funds

## Team
- [ ] Org chart and key hires plan
- [ ] Founder and employee agreements; IP assignment

## Legal and IP
- [ ] Registered IP and assignments
- [ ] Material licences, permits and regulatory approvals
- [ ] Any litigation or disputes
