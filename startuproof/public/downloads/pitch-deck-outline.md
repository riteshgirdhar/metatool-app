# Pitch deck outline (12 slides)

Each slide should answer one question.

1. **Title** — Who are you and what do you do, in one line?
2. **Problem** — Who has the problem, how often, and what does it cost them?
3. **Solution** — What do you do about it, and why is it better than today's workaround?
4. **Product** — What does it look like? (Screens, not adjectives.)
5. **Market** — How many customers like your best customers exist, and what would they pay? Show the calculation.
6. **Traction** — What evidence do you have? Metrics with definitions and time periods.
7. **Business model** — How do you make money? Unit economics if you have them.
8. **Go-to-market** — How do you reach customers, and what has worked so far?
9. **Competition** — What else do customers use, and why do they choose you?
10. **Team** — Why are you the people to build this?
11. **Financials** — Where are you now, and what are the next 18–24 months?
12. **The ask** — How much, on what instrument, and what milestones will it fund?
