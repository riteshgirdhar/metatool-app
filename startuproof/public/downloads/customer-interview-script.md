# Customer discovery interview script

**Goal:** learn how often the problem happens, what it costs, and what people do today. Do not pitch.

## Before the call
- Segment (be specific): ____
- Hypothesis being tested: ____

## Opening (2 min)
"Thanks for your time. I'm researching how [role] handle [job]. There are no right answers — I'm interested in what actually happened, not opinions about a product."

## Questions (20–25 min)
1. Tell me about the last time you had to [do the job]. *(Stay quiet; let them talk.)*
2. What was the hardest part? Why was that hard?
3. How often does this come up?
4. What did you try to solve it? What did that cost — time, money, risk?
5. Who else was involved? Who approves spending on this?
6. What happens if it doesn't get solved?
7. Is there anyone else you think I should speak to?

Avoid: "Would you use…?", "How much would you pay…?", or describing your idea.
