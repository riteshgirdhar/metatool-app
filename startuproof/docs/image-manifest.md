## Adding real photography

1. Put originals in `src/assets/photos/` (not `public/`) with a row here: source, licence, photographer credit,
   consent/model release for identifiable people, and whether it is **genuine** (Startup Roof event/member),
   **stock**, or **conceptual**.
2. Use Astro's `<Picture>` from `astro:assets` with `formats={['avif','webp']}`, `widths={[480, 768, 1200, 1600]}`,
   a `sizes` attribute, meaningful `alt`, and `loading="eager" fetchpriority="high"` **only** for the hero/LCP image.
3. Keep the mobile hero ≤ ~200 KB (AVIF at the 768 w variant usually achieves this).
4. Never caption stock models as members, and never present renderings as photographs of a confirmed venue.
